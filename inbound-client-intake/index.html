If yes: Great! As you know, this policy will be with you for the rest of your life. It doesn't expire regardless of age, and your monthly payments will stay locked in, so you won't have to worry about your premiums increasing or the benefits decreasing. Your coverage and payments are guaranteed.

If no: No worries! A whole life policy will be with you for the rest of your life. It doesn't expire regardless of age, and your monthly payments will stay locked in, so you won't have to worry about your premiums increasing or the benefits decreasing. Your coverage and payments are guaranteed. Does that make sense?

Great. Please write down these three things:

1. Permanent protection — the policy is designed to remain in force for your lifetime as long as the required premiums are paid.

2. Level premium and fixed rate — your scheduled premium stays the same and your approved rate is locked in.

3. Day-one coverage goal — my goal is to get you day-one coverage. If the carrier approves immediate coverage, the policy is issued, and the initial payment processes, your protection begins on the policy's effective date.

The death benefit is generally paid income-tax-free directly to your named beneficiary, which normally keeps it out of probate.

Only discuss living benefits when health and the approved carrier and policy qualify; typically do not use this for Ethos unless the approval confirms it

If living benefits are included and you qualify, you may be able to access a portion of the policy benefit after a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness.

Cash value — only if the client asks

These whole life policies build cash value over time. The cash value grows tax-deferred and may be accessed for emergencies such as chronic illness or disease, subject to the carrier's policy terms. A portion of the premium supports the policy's cash value, where it can grow according to the policy.

Only state that cash value can be withdrawn after two years when the approved carrier and policy confirm that access point

Does that all make sense?
