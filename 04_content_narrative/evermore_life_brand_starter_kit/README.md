# Evermore LIFE Brand Starter Kit

**Status:** local draft, not published
**Scope:** five core assets modeled on the AwardSignal brand-kit structure
**Source of truth:** `01_website/v2/assets/evermorelife-llc-logo-nav.png` and the existing Evermore recruiting language in `01_website/v2/pages/recruiting.html`

## Core assets

1. `evermore-life-primary-lockup.svg` — primary logo lockup on a light surface
2. `evermore-life-profile-mark.svg` — compact profile/avatar mark
3. `evermore-life-social-cover.svg` — 1512 × 256 social/company cover
4. `evermore-life-social-square.svg` — 1080 × 1080 feed post
5. `evermore-life-social-story.svg` — 1080 × 1920 story/reel cover

Open `preview.html` for a visual review of all five assets.

These are editable SVG masters. They intentionally reuse the existing canonical
Evermore logo and do not introduce new claims, carrier promises, testimonials,
or live publishing. PNG derivatives and additional platform placements remain
out of scope for this starter pass.
