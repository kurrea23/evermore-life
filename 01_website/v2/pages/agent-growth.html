Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Advertising spend is funded separately by the client. Evermore does not disguise ad spend as a management fee.

How much should I spend on advertising?

Evermore Growth is designed around a recommended starting investment of at least $100/day. Established teams may be better suited for $300/day or more depending on market, campaign structure, economics, and capacity.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Advertising performance, appointments, applications, insurance production, profitability, and financial results cannot be guaranteed. Evermore builds, tests, and optimizes acquisition infrastructure toward agreed objectives.

Is GoHighLevel included?

Applicable Evermore-managed CRM infrastructure is included according to the selected package. Usage-based services may carry additional charges.

Do I own my website?

Growth and Team OS include custom web infrastructure. Ownership, hosting, deployment, transfer, and termination terms are governed by the applicable service agreement and third-party platform requirements.

What does “own your agency assets” mean?

Growth and Team OS are designed to build appropriate durable assets around the client’s agency, subject to contractual and third-party platform terms. It does not mean the client owns third-party platforms such as GoHighLevel, Meta, hosting infrastructure, AI providers, or other licensed technology.

Can my team use the system?

Yes. Team OS is specifically designed around multi-agent operations.

Does Evermore close my insurance sales?

No. Evermore creates and manages acquisition infrastructure. Appropriately licensed producers remain responsible for insurance recommendations, applications, and sales.

How quickly can we launch?

Timing depends on account access, advertising-platform approvals, A2P registration where applicable, creative production, domain configuration, client responsiveness, and platform review. No unsupported guaranteed timeline is promised.
