08 · Usage & governance

Warm, credible, and disciplined.

A consistent brand protects trust. Use the supplied files, preserve the evidence boundary, and give every message enough space to feel calm.

Do
  • Use the primary logo wherever space permits.
  • Keep clear space equal to the “L” cap height.
  • Use gold for the primary next step and meaningful emphasis.
  • Use warm, authentic imagery with room for copy and crops.
  • State uncertainty, eligibility, and underwriting conditions plainly.
Do not
  • Redraw, stretch, rotate, crop, outline, or recolor the logo.
  • Place the mark over faces, clutter, or low-contrast photography.
  • Use fear, shame, false scarcity, or guaranteed-outcome language.
  • Use fake customers, testimonials, carrier seals, or invented proof.
  • Publish templates containing placeholder names, links, or contact details.
E

Before anything goes live

Confirm the destination, contact details, state availability, carrier/product facts, consent language, and final crop. Templates are starting points—not permission to publish unchecked information.
