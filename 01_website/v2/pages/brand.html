04 · Usage

Warm, credible, and evidence-aware.

Do

  • Use the supplied logo artwork as provided.
  • Keep generous clear space around the mark.
  • Use gold for primary actions and emphasis.
  • Keep product and policy language accurate.

Do not

  • Stretch, redraw, rotate, or recolor the logo.
  • Use hype, guaranteed outcomes, or unsupported claims.
  • Use fake testimonials or invented customer proof.
  • Publish an unreviewed asset as final.
